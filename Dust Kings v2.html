How to play

Keyboard

  • ↑ / W gas · starts the clock
  • ↓ / S brake · hold when stopped to reverse
  • ← / A nose up in the air
  • → / D nose down in the air
  • E / Q shift up / down (sequential, manual mode)
  • R reset to the last safe spot (+5 s)
  • Esc pause · M mute

Touch

  • GAS and BRAKE on the right
  • ⟲ ⟳ on the left rotate the truck in the air
  • Pause button top right

Reading the course

  • Yellow !, orange !! and red !!! signs warn of hazards. The callout at the top counts down the distance.
  • Land with the gas off or you can snap an axle.
  • Deep water drowns the engine. A snorkel keeps the intake dry.

Winning

  • Career: finish stages to unlock the next one. Beat the silver and gold times for more stars and cash.
  • Spend money in the Dealership and Garage. Parts change power, weight, travel, heat and how the truck sounds.
  • After a run, the Auto-tune tab reads your telemetry and suggests suspension and gearing changes.
  • Damage stays on the truck. Repair it in the garage before a long stage.
